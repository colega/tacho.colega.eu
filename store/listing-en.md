Tacho Colega — tachograph card

## Short description (max 80 chars)
Download your EU tachograph card over NFC. Timeline, calendar and driving-time insights.

## Full description
Tacho Colega turns your European tachograph card into something you can actually use.

Hold your driver card against the back of your NFC phone and the app reads every file
on it — no cables, no PC, no workshop visit.

WHAT YOU GET

• Card overview — holder, card number, validity dates, current session, card version.
• Timeline — each day as a colour-coded 24-hour bar: driving, work, availability, break, rest.
• Calendar — a month view where every day is coloured by how much you drove.
• Insights — your driving totals against the EU limits and a clear list of any rule
  violations, with how many minutes over you were.
• Export — save the complete card contents to a file you can keep, print or send.

CHECKED AGAINST EU RULES (561/2006)

• 9 h daily driving limit (10 h twice a week)
• 56 h weekly / 90 h two-weekly driving limits
• 45-minute break after 4.5 h driving, including the 15 + 30 minute split
• Daily and weekly rest

WORKS WITH

• Driver tachograph cards, generation 1 and generation 2 (smart tachograph)
• The 2021 amendment layout: border crossings, load/unload operations and GNSS
  position files
• Any phone with NFC (Android 8.0+)

PRIVACY

Your card data never leaves the phone unless you export it yourself. No account,
no tracking, no uploads.

Note: Tacho Colega is an independent tool for drivers. It is not official equipment
under Regulation (EU) 165/2014 and does not replace the vehicle unit or any official
record.
