Everything on the card, in plain language

The app reads the card the way inspectors do — every elementary file, byte by byte — then shows what matters to you.

Full card download

Every file: identity, driving licence, events, faults, daily activity, vehicles, places and the version 2 files for border crossings and load operations.

Daily timeline

Each day as a colour-coded 24-hour bar: driving, work, availability, breaks and rest — exactly as the card recorded it.

Month calendar

Scroll through the weeks and see at a glance how the month went: green for normal days, amber for extended ones, red where you went over.

EU rules, checked

Daily and weekly driving limits, the 4h30/45-minute break rule with the 15+30 split, two-week totals — each breach flagged with the minutes over.

Export & archive

Save the complete card contents to a portable file you can keep, print or send to your employer or an inspector.

Checked against Regulation (EC) No 561/2006

The same limits enforcement officers apply, applied to your own data.

Card data is parsed and stored on your device. Uploads only happen when you export or share a file yourself.

Independent tool

Tacho Colega is not official equipment under Regulation (EU) 165/2014; it complements the vehicle unit, never replaces it.
