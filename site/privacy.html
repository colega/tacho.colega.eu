Privacy policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

This policy describes how the Tacolega Android app handles information from a driver tachograph card.

Developer and contact

Tacolega is maintained by Colega. For privacy questions, use the project issue tracker. Issues are public. Do not post card files, card numbers, or other personal card data there.

Card data the app handles

When you read a card with a compatible USB CCID reader or open a card file, Tacolega reads and processes the data on your phone. Depending on the card, this can include the cardholder's name and card number, driving licence details, activity records, vehicle registrations, events and faults, and GNSS position records stored on the card.

Local storage and retention

Tacolega saves the latest card dump in the app's private storage so it can restore the card view after a restart. The saved dump is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. Tacolega has no account system, analytics, advertising, or server that receives card data.

The saved dump remains on the phone until another card is read or imported, or you clear Tacolega's app storage or uninstall the app. The original file you open remains wherever you stored it.

Export and sharing

If you choose Export, Android opens the share sheet and sends a copy of the card dump to the app or destination you select. That app or service handles the copy under its own privacy policy. Files you export or share are outside Tacolega's control.

Security

The saved dump is kept in Android's app-private storage. Tacolega does not transmit it to a Tacolega server. Protect your phone with the device security options available to you.

Deleting your data

To delete the saved copy, clear Tacolega's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. Delete exported copies separately in the apps or services where you saved or shared them.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page if the app's data handling changes. The date above shows the latest revision.
